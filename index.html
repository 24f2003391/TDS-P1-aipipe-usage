Interpretation: spend and provider totals cover all included email domains; IITM users are a separate filtered user count. The feed represents positive-cost metered usage, not every AI Pipe interaction. Free/zero-cost requests and direct provider-key traffic may be absent. The current day is not included until the source marks it complete. Provider residuals near zero are normalized for display. Rolling user metrics are averages or ratios of daily counts, not unique people across the full window. No individual IDs or per-email-domain breakdowns are available in this feed.
